About

Not just a developer.
A whole perspective.

I'm Charu — and I've always cared more about what something feels like than what it technically does. That instinct started in competitive speaking — elocution, MUNs, book reviews — and never left when I switched to code.

Now I build across AI, full-stack, and systems design, with a particular interest in anything at the intersection of language and technology. Interned at Vicharanashala, IIT Ropar under Prof. Sudharshan Iyengar, where I built the Speech Confidence Scorer for Spandan — a live classroom AI platform. Currently preparing for the placement season.

I iterate toward simplicity, not away from complexity. I build fast, refine by instinct — and always pull back until it's clean.

◈ What I care about Systems honest about what they can't do. Interfaces that don't waste attention. Code that reads like good prose.
